Printing

  1. Print at 100% / Actual size. Turn off “Fit to page” / “Scale to fit”.
  2. For a 4×6 or 5×7 sheet, use that photo paper, or order a regular 4×6 print at a drugstore (CVS, Walgreens, Walmart).
  3. After printing, measure one photo with a ruler before cutting to confirm the size.
  4. Cut along the thin gray lines.
